## Model (scaled units; for the methods text of `sec:lever-noise`, two or three sentences)

**q1.** Deployed as m2 (no run-path change): q^n = scale_out(c [x^n; 1] + η^n), η^n = μ + z^n,
z^n = Φ1 z^{n-1} + Φ2 z^{n-2} + ξ^n, ξ^n ~ N(0, Σ_ξ), Φ diagonal, restricted φ1 ≥ 0, φ2 ≤ 0 (rule of 2026-10-07).
- **Training noise.** As Eq. `eq: window noise`, with the window's draws η^{n-1}, …, η^{n-h} a path of the deployed AR
  instead of i.i.d. N(0, Σ). The expected squared error is again a ridge: c' = (X'X + N S_dx) \ X'Y, S_dx = M W M',
  W the block-Toeplitz covariance of the window's draws, block (j, j') = D Γ^z_{j'-j} D, Γ^z_k the deployed AR's lagged
  covariance (exact: stationary covariance of the companion form by a discrete Lyapunov solve, then
  Γ_k = Φ1 Γ_{k-1} + Φ2 Γ_{k-2}). With Φ = 0 and Σ_ξ = Σ this is `lrs_noise_variant.jl`'s S_dx = M (I_h ⊗ DΣD) M'.
- **Residual given the noisy history** (as m2): e^n = r^n − C'M w^n, lagged covariances
  Γ^e_k = (1/N) Σ_n r^n r^{n-k}' + C'M W_k M'C, W_k = E[w^n w^{n-k}'] (for AR draws nonzero at every lag; for white
  draws only k < h, which is `fit_ar_noisy`).
- **Noise model** (as m2): φ per QoI by `ar2_ls` on Γ^e_k[i,i]/Γ^e_0[i,i], k = 1–20, restricted; Σ_ξ = D R D, R the
  correlation of the exact innovation covariance, D matching the AR's marginal variance to diag Γ^e_0; Σ (stored
  `stoch_distr`, used for μ only) = Γ^e_0, μ = mean(r).
- **Fixed point** over (c, Σ, φ, Σ_ξ), started from m2's (φ, Σ_ξ); stop when φ repeats exactly and Σ_ξ changes by
  < 1e-8 relative. (`ar2_ls` is a grid + refinement, so φ moves on a ~2e-4 lattice; a 2-cycle is reported, not hidden.
  Not 1e-12 as for LinReg1_nt: with the AR the map has a rounding floor of ~2e-9, from the normal-equations solve at
  cond(X'X) 3e12, the q* block of gotcha #13; deterministic, contraction ~0.7 above the floor.)
- Fit rows: LinReg1's own, found by reproducing its c (405–3999, as LinReg1_nt). 🔒 Nothing past 10 TU.
- Approximations, as step 2b and m2: only the mean's response to the draws is modelled (its own errors feeding back
  are not); the draws are independent of the record. New here: online the AR draw η^n is correlated with the draws in
  its window; the training sees that only through the residual's ACF.

**q2.** Deployed as `LinReg7_ar2n_pl` (no run-path change): η^n = μ + f(q*^n) ⊙ z^n, f_i = (clamp(q*_i)/q_ref,i)^β_i.
- Training noise: the draw η^{n-j} carries the scale of its own step, f(q*^{n-j}) at the RECORDED predictor (the
  history's perturbation of q* is ignored in f). The noise covariance then changes from row to row; summed over rows,
  Σ_n S_dx,n = M (W^z ⊙ Σ_n g_n g_n') M', g_n = [D f(q*^{n-1}); …; D f(q*^{n-h})]: still closed form.
- β and the variance at q_ref by maximum likelihood (`RF.fit_powerlaw_scale`) on the expected squared residual
  E[(e^n_i)²] = (r^n_i)² + (A_n W^z A_n')_ii, A_n = C'M diag(g_n): the exact expected NLL (it is linear in e²).
  q_ref and the clip range from q* on the fit rows (the clip never binds there).
- AR on e/f with exact row-dependent lagged covariances; Σ_ε = Γ^{e/f}_0 (diag Σ_ε = exp(logvar), asserted).
- Fixed point over (c, β, logvar, φ, Σ_ξ). With β = 0 q2 is q1 exactly (tested).

**Methods text for the paper session** (draft, for the end of `sec:lever-noise`; not written into the paper):

> The mean model can also be trained with the noise the AR(2) closure draws. The window's draws then follow an AR(2)
> fitted, as above, to the residual given the noisy history; the penalty is the covariance of the perturbation under
> these draws, and the fit, $\Sigma$ and the AR(2) are iterated to a fixed point. The closure deploys that AR(2). A
> second variant also scales the draws by the power law of step 2, in training and in deployment.

## Decisions for Rik

- **D1 — q1's offline stop rule (proposed).** Per QoI, exact (no draws), q1 against m2 (`analysis/noise_ar_offline.jl`):
  (a) the gain, the response per unit draw under the closure's own AR draws, sqrt(diag(C S_dx C') / drawn variance);
  (b) the drawn AR's long-run variance ratio, 1 + 2 Σ_{k=1}^{200} ρ_AR(k) (App. `app:ar-fit`'s measure).
  Both are properties of the fitted closure, the same on any rows. m2's (2026-11-06, the partner's, already scored):
  gain 0.54 0.45 0.79 0.94 0.41 0.53; AR long-run variance ratio 2.31 2.16 2.51 2.96 1.69 1.99

  **If both lie within 10 % of m2's in all six QoIs, q1 is reported offline only** (one sentence in step 2b) and its
  coupled runs are skipped: its coupled outcome would be m2's. Otherwise q1 runs. If q1 stops, q2 stops too (it would be
  m2 + power law, which was cut; C3 predicts its effect).
- **D2 — q2's β.** Proposed: refit given the noisy history, inside the fixed point (the rule Σ and the AR already
  follow, Rik 2026-11-04: "same way"). Alternative: keep LinReg1_pl's β (then the scale is fitted to a residual the
  closure does not meet).
